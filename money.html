Monthly money plan

This page is for when you are earning about $700 a week in the hand. Every dollar has a job. Follow it each payday and the rent debt goes down every week.

Set it up once

You have three accounts. Give each one a job, then make the payments automatic.

AccountIts jobEach payday
Original (pay lands here)Bills only. Never spend from it.$558
Replacement (your card)Gas, groceries, fun$130
SavingsCar fund and buffer$12

Once these run, the only money you can spend is the $130 on your card. Your rent and phone are already paid.

Every payday, in this order

  1. Fill the tank. $70.
  2. Check the rent went out.
  3. Supermarket shop. $45. Use free meals and food parcels to top it up.
  4. The $15 left is yours.

This month

Tick each week off. After week 4, press the button to start a new month.

After four weeks you will have: paid $2,040 rent in full, taken $120 off your debt, and put $48 in Savings.
