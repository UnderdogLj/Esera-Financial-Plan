Monthly money plan

You can fix this. Do one small job a day. Tick it off when it is done. Your ticks are saved on this phone.

Every payday, do these 3 things first

  1. Gas first. Put $70 in the tank on the way home. Now you can get to work all week.
  2. Rent second. Send your rent straight away. A part payment still counts.
  3. Food third. One supermarket shop, about $45. Use free meals and food parcels to top it up.

What is left is yours. Keep $15 for a treat.

Week 1: one job a day

Each job takes 30 minutes or less. Missed a day? Do it tomorrow.

Calling Work and Income

Free call. You can get help even if you are working part time.

0800 559 009

Say this:

Hi. I work part time but I do not get enough hours. I am behind on my rent and I might lose my home. I want to apply for three things: Jobseeker Support, the Accommodation Supplement, and help with my rent arrears. Can you please note today as my first contact date?

Then ask: "Is there any help with travel costs so I can get to work?"

Have these ready. Photos on your phone are fine.

Rent arrears help is up to 4 weeks of rent. It goes straight to your landlord and you pay it back slowly. Write down the name of the person you spoke to and the date. Finish the application within 20 working days of your first call.

Message for your landlord

Staying silent is what gets people evicted. Copy this, fill in the gaps, and send it.

Hi, this is [your name] from [your unit number]. I know I am behind on my rent and I want to fix it. Can you please send me my rent ledger so I know the exact amount? I have contacted Work and Income for help with the arrears. From [date] I can pay $[amount] every payday, plus $[amount] a week off what I owe. Can we agree to this in writing? Thank you.

Only promise what you can really pay. Open every letter. If you get a Tenancy Tribunal notice, turn up.
